# FITVELY Diet Video Observation Sheet

Date: 2026-05-25

## Problem

FITVELY nutrition exact-video Flows had the right one-action shape, but they were still classified as memo-first and their detail copy compressed source-rule selection into a vague application instruction.

## Scope

- Treat FITVELY diet exact-video routes as sheet-first observation Flows.
- Keep one action per video.
- Add visible `기준 후보`, `적용 기준`, `관찰표`, `원본 영상`, `기록`, and `중단 조건` copy.
- Preserve source boundaries and avoid inventing video-specific nutrition values.

## Out Of Scope

- Automatic diet plans.
- Gram targets or nutrient calculations.
- Health outcomes or weight-loss promises.
- Workout-plan video restructuring.
- Figma layout work.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Covered FITVELY diet exact-video routes infer `sheet` as primary destination.
- Each detail panel includes source-rule candidate selection and observation-sheet guidance.
- Existing Playwright checks for diet exact-video pages still pass.
- No route is called validated.
