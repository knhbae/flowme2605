## Sequence

1. Recheck the current repository and isolate pre-existing dirty paths.
2. Normalize the seven relevant platform mechanisms from the 22-platform
   dossier.
3. Group all 24 verified vertical services into the eight existing execution
   patterns and separate observed behavior from inference and unknowns.
4. Map the evidence to the existing canonical ownership and version contract.
5. Compare operating alternatives and write the recommended hybrid policy.
6. Define field ownership, conflict resolution, representative scenarios, and
   a proposed observed-session protocol.
7. Build a quantitative evidence ledger, evidence dashboard, eight real-service
   anatomy cards, and four code-native UI contract mockups.
8. Produce machine-readable matrices, Korean executive report, and team
   handoff.
9. Parse JSON, check links/docs, inspect the report at wide and mobile
   viewports, and record scoped closeout.

## Risk Controls

- Existing dirty files remain unowned and are not reverted, staged, or folded
  into this package.
- External platform outcomes are not treated as FlowMe adoption evidence.
- Public product descriptions do not prove signed-in behavior, long-term use,
  external write-back, or conflict handling.
- Existing canonical contracts and new product hypotheses are labeled
  separately.
- The strategy package does not alter runtime, seed, schema, or export logic.
- No commit, push, PR, merge, or deployment is included.
