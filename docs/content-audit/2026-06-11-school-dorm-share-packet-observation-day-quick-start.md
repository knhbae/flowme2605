## Say This

Use short neutral prompts:

- "이건 제품 설명이 아니라 공유 패킷 이해 확인입니다."
- "이 패킷만 보고 가장 먼저 무엇을 확인할지 말해주세요."
- "학교나 기숙사의 최신 공지와 이 패킷이 다르면 어느 쪽을 우선할까요?"
- "여기에 적으면 안 되는 값은 무엇이라고 보이세요?"
- "FlowMe가 직접 전송하거나 댓글을 모으는 것으로 보이세요, 아니면 사람이 복사해서 전달하는 것으로 보이세요?"

## Do Not Say Or Record

- Do not explain the FlowMe roadmap before the task.
- Do not teach the labels before the recipient tries.
- Do not record real names, phone numbers, room numbers, passwords, keys, payments, health records, student IDs, resident IDs, invite links, form edit links, webhook URLs, or chat logs.
- Do not record exact private quotes if they contain sensitive details. Record the risk category only.
- Do not call a session `validated`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop the session and write only the risk category if the recipient:

- is not attached to an `agreed` role-labeled slot in the scheduling tracker;
- expects FlowMe to find room, password, key, or private notice details;
- tries to write health, payment, identity, room, channel, invite, form edit, webhook, or chat-log values;
- expects FlowMe to send, post, collect replies, store contacts, or track channels automatically;
- cannot tell that the latest school/dorm notice overrides the packet.

If the same stop condition happens twice, revise the packet before more sessions.

## End-Of-Day Handoff

Do not make a product decision from memory. End the day by producing:

- one filled pilot worksheet;
- one session log with role labels only;
- one rollup draft choosing exactly `no signal`, `friction`, or `candidate signal`;
- one next action from the post-rollup decision guide.

Until that handoff exists, the evidence board remains `no signal`.
