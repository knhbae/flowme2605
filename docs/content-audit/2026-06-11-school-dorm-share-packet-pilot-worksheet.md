## Use Rules

- Use role labels only.
- Do not record real names.
- Do not record health, payment, student identity, resident identity, room, channel, invite, form edit, webhook, or chat-log values.
- Record only `pass`, `fail`, or `unclear` for checks.
- Keep `FlowMe explained before task` as `no`.
- Keep a session status as `not run` unless the matching role-labeled scheduling slot is `agreed`.
- If private details appear, stop the session and write only the risk category.

## Stop And Revise

Stop the pilot and revise the packet before more sessions if any of these happen twice:

- participant thinks FlowMe finds room, password, key, or private notice details;
- participant tries to record health, payment, identity, room, channel, invite, form edit, webhook, or chat-log values;
- participant expects FlowMe to send, post, collect replies, store contacts, or track channels;
- participant cannot tell that latest school/dorm notice overrides the packet.
