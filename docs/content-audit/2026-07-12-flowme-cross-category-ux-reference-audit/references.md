# Official references reviewed

Checked: 2026-07-12

## Calendar and schedule

| Product | Official reference | Pattern reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Calendar | [Change how you view events on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/guide/iphone/iphfd1054569/ios) | Month view can switch between compact, stacked, and detailed density; list view owns readable event detail. |
| Google Calendar | [View your day, week, or month](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/6110849?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en) | Month is navigation; Schedule is the day-grouped readable list. |
| Google Calendar | [Google Calendar Help](https://support.google.com/calendar/?hl=en) | Events, tasks, calendar visibility, density, and import/export are separate controls around one schedule model. |
| Fantastical | [Product overview](https://flexibits.com/fantastical?level=1) | DayTicker gives a quick overview while full day/week/month views expose more detail; tasks sit alongside events. |
| Fantastical | [Calendar Views](https://flexibits.com/fantastical/help/calendar-views) | Mini window supports quick input and upcoming list; full window owns broad calendar navigation. |
| Notion Calendar | [Product overview](https://www.notion.com/product/calendar) | Work deadlines and events appear together; dates can be adjusted directly without moving to a separate admin surface. |
